# Veterinary sources and simulation design notes

The CSV generated by this package contains **synthetic examples only**. No row is a real animal record. These sources informed broad symptom relationships and a few qualitative context associations; they do not provide the numerical probabilities used by the simulator. The rates in `generate_dataset.py` are explicit engineering assumptions for creating overlap and variability, not published prevalence estimates.

## Translation into synthetic assumptions

- The symptom profiles are severity-conditioned Bernoulli probabilities with additional row-level variation. They deliberately allow characteristic signs to be absent and common signs to overlap across diseases.
- The Healthy class has a low but nonzero chance of isolated mild observations. Severe symptom combinations remain infrequent by construction.
- Vaccination status overlaps between healthy animals and all disease classes; vaccination lowers its simulated frequency in the corresponding disease class but does not eliminate breakthrough cases. The numeric effect sizes are assumptions, not efficacy estimates.
- FMD is not restricted to one species. HS has a soft monsoon/humid context association. LSD has no buffalo exclusion. Blackleg age is drawn with a younger-stock tendency; that age prior is a design choice needing local clinical validation. Mastitis is concentrated among mature female cattle/buffalo.
- The clinical features are symptom proxies, not diagnostic tests. Many real differentials and confirmatory laboratory tests are absent because the requested target schema has exactly seven classes and exactly 32 input features.

## Limitations

The published sources support qualitative clinical relationships, not the simulator's class priors, conditional probabilities, sampling frame, or synthetic model performance. A model trained and tested on samples from this simulator can only demonstrate that the code learns the simulator. It cannot establish clinical accuracy or calibrated real-world confidence. Before clinical use, replace or supplement synthetic rows with independently collected, veterinarian-labelled records and perform prospective external validation and recalibration.
